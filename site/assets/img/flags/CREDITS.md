## A note on the Afghanistan flag

`af.svg` is the **black, red and green tricolour of the Islamic Republic**, in
use from 2013 to 2021 — not the white flag flown by the government in place
since August 2021.

This is a choice, not an oversight. It is the flag used by most of the Afghan
diaspora, including by many of the women this archive exists to record, some of
whom left precisely because of the change in government. Showing the current
state flag beside their accounts would, for some of them, be painful.

It is also a political choice, and it is yours to make rather than mine. If you
want the current flag instead, replace this one file — nothing else refers to it
by name.

## Do not "optimise" these

An earlier pass at stripping editor metadata from these files also removed
standalone `x="…"` attributes, which are real SVG positioning attributes. It
silently deleted Uzbekistan's crescent and stars. The files are small enough as
they are; leave them alone.
